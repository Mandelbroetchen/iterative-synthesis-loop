## 5. Mathematical Model

### 5.1 Spacetime Curvature
The curvature at any point (x, y) is calculated as:

C(x, y) = sum over all objects of (G * m_i) / (r_i^2 + epsilon)

Where:
- G is the gravitational constant
- m_i is the mass of object i
- r_i is the distance from point (x, y) to object i
- epsilon is a small constant to prevent division by zero

### 5.2 Object Movement
Object positions are updated based on gravitational forces:

F = sum over all other objects of (G * m_i * m_j) / (r_ij^2) * direction_vector

Where:
- F is the net force on an object
- m_i, m_j are masses of interacting objects
- r_ij is the distance between objects i and j
